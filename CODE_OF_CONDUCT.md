# AEP AI Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AEP AI community respectful, welcoming,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and technically honest.
- Respect differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback and focus on what is best for the community.
- Protect private information and report safety concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, or publishing another person's private information is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[opensource@aepai.org](mailto:opensource@aepai.org). Maintainers may remove
content or participation that violates this Code. Enforcement decisions are
handled privately and are not part of public security operations. General
project information is available at [aepai.org](https://aepai.org).
